📍 Batanes, Philippines

About the Place

Batanes is known for its beautiful landscapes, rolling hills, scenic coastlines, traditional stone houses, and peaceful surroundings. It is a destination where visitors can enjoy nature while experiencing the unique culture of the Ivatan people.
