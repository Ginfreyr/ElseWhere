Mount Pulag

📍 Benguet, Philippines

About the Place

Mount Pulag is the highest mountain in Luzon and one of the most popular hiking destinations in the Philippines. It is known for its beautiful landscapes, cool weather, and breathtaking sea of clouds.

Travel Tips

  • Bring warm clothing because the temperature can be cold.
  • Pack enough water and snacks for the hike.
  • Check weather conditions before traveling.
  • Follow hiking rules and designated trails.
  • Leave no trace and keep the mountain clean.
